# Setting

The world of Adastrea as the user has described it. Design decisions should agree with this page;
if one doesn't, raise it with the user instead of quietly bending either.

## The frontier (2026-10-02)

- The year is unknown.
- The game is set in remote galaxies, far from where the great empires fought their endless wars.
  Those wars left few people alive.
- **People are scarce.** In this remote region every person is worth a great deal: as crew, as
  labour, as a citizen, as a customer.
- Because of that, **nobody kills unless they have to**, not even pirates. Disabling a ship and
  taking its people is always worth more than killing them.

## What follows from it

- Ships are disabled into wrecks rather than destroyed; crews eject in pods; stations send drones
  to tow the disabled home. All of it exists to keep people alive.
- Taking a life is the worst crime there is, for lawful people and pirates alike.
- See `11-TECHNICAL_SPECS/PIRACY_AND_LAW.md` for how this shapes combat, piracy and capture.
